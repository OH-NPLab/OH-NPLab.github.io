Start a conversation with the Nutritional Psychology Lab.

The Nutritional Psychology Lab (NPL) at Tel-Hai University welcomes enquiries from prospective MA and PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and researchers interested in collaboration around nutritional psychology and our related research themes.

The lab is headed by Prof. Omer Horovitz . You can explore our research areas and publications before getting in touch.
